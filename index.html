The principles

Restraint is a feature, not an absence.

Most banking apps are designed to be opened. Sable is designed to be closed — because the account is already doing what you asked of it.

01

Settled overnight

Balances reconcile at 04:00 ET against a live ledger. What you see at breakfast is what cleared, not what's pending.

02

Thirty-one currencies

03
